Cognitive / continuity lens

Compare the dream with waking-life concerns, people, goals and feelings. This is one possible lens, not the answer.

Simple idea: dreams often use material that is continuous with waking concerns and conceptions. That does not mean every dream has a clear waking-life match, or that a dream proves anything about you.
Keep it groundedSafety
  • A dream cannot prove that an event happened, diagnose you, or verify a memory.
  • Violent or disturbing dream content is not automatically a waking intention.
  • Do not make a major medical, legal, financial, safety or relationship decision because of a dream alone.
  • If you are having trouble telling dream material from waking reality, stop the lens and focus on waking support.
  • You can leave at any time. Nothing in this lens changes the original dream.

    Your data

    Simple saving, backup and restore. Dream Catcher itself sends nothing to the internet.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN

    Dream Catcher stores your dreams only in this browser on this device

    Dream Catcher has no account, no server and no tracking. It cannot see or upload your dreams. Your phone/browser may have its own backup or sync settings; Dream Catcher does not control those.

    • Normal use: tap Save dream. The dream is stored inside this browser on this device.
    • Nothing is saved while you are only typing. Saving happens when you choose to tap Save dream.
    • A backup is different. A backup is a separate file created only when you tap Download backup file.

    Everyday use

    1. Open the same dream-catcher.html file in the same browser.
    2. Tap Capture, record the dream, then tap Save dream.
    3. Next time, open the same file and tap Journal. Your saved dreams should be there.

    Important: browser data can be cleared by you, the browser or the phone. If the dreams matter, make a backup.

    1. Make a backup

    This makes one readable .json file containing your dreams and any optional personal context you saved. Do this before changing phones, browsers, HTML versions, or clearing browser data.

    Dream Catcher does not choose a cloud service. Your phone/browser decides where the downloaded file goes. If you save it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or another cloud folder, that backup copy may sync there.

    2. Load a backup

    Use this to restore dreams into this copy of Dream Catcher.

    Advanced restore choice
    If unsure, use Replace. Make a fresh backup first if this copy already contains dreams you want to keep.

    iPhone / iPad

    To save a backup: tap Download backup file. In Safari, use the Downloads button or open the Files app and look in Downloads.

    To load it: tap Choose your Dream Catcher backup file, find the .json file in Files, select it, then tap Load backup.

    If the backup is in iCloud Drive, that file can sync through iCloud. If you want a device-only backup, choose an on-device Files location when your phone offers one.

    Android

    To save a backup: tap Download backup file. Chrome normally puts downloaded files in Downloads. Open your Files app and look in Downloads.

    To load it: tap Choose your Dream Catcher backup file, choose the .json file from Files/Downloads, then tap Load backup.

    If you move the backup to Google Drive or another cloud folder, that copy may sync there. Dream Catcher itself does not upload it.

    When should I make a backup?

    • Before installing/opening a newer Dream Catcher HTML file.
    • Before changing browser or phone.
    • Before clearing browser/site data.
    • Whenever you would be upset to lose the dreams you have recorded.
